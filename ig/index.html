twilpaw Introducing Twilpaw.

We started close to the fabric, the stitching, and the fit, and with the people who share their days with dogs and cats.

What they taught us shaped everything: fit comes before fashion, materials should hold up to real life, and a good piece is one that gets worn again and again. No throwaway pieces.

Twilpaw is apparel and accessories for dogs and cats. Warm light, quiet rooms, considered pieces. Our first collection is on the way — starting with everyday harnesses, leashes, and collars made to move with them.

To our first followers: thank you for finding us early. A welcome discount is waiting for you when our first collection arrives — and you'll hear about it here first.
